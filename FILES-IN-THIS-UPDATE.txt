THE BLANK ROLL
==============

Your driver was set to 80 x 3276 mm. A thermal printer feeds to the end of the
page its driver is set to, and the browser cannot override that: Chrome only
offers the paper sizes the driver exposes, so asking for a 104mm page is
ignored unless a form that size exists on the machine.

Two things in this update:

  * Printing → Page length, so the browser's page matches the form the printer
    is actually on. Nothing is scaled or split.
  * DEPLOY-WINDOWS-OFFLINE.md now has the steps to create a Windows form of
    80 x 120 mm, after which the printer feeds only that much. That is the
    only way to remove the waste entirely short of talking ESC/POS to the
    printer directly.

Also: Chrome's Margins → None and Headers and footers → off, once per machine.
The date and "localhost:5173" at the top of your first receipts were Chrome's
headers, not ours.
